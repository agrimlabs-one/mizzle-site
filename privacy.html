Mizzle: Privacy Policy

Mizzle does not collect, store or share any personal information.

The game does not use the internet, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. The only data it keeps is your current level and your sound setting, saved on your own device and never sent anywhere.

The optional one-time purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Mizzle never sees your payment details or Apple ID; it only learns from Apple whether the purchase has been made.
